## Week 4 Portal Submission Description

This Week 4 assessment presents a Production Planning and Scheduling Analysis in SAP SCM using a practical internship simulation for the fictional Deccan Harvest Foods Pvt. Ltd. The project connects demand planning with finite-capacity production scheduling, SAP SCM/APO planning concepts, master data, bottleneck analysis, disruption recovery, KPI monitoring, and validation controls.

The scenario models peak-week demand of 138.5 tonnes against a nominal packaging capacity of 145 tonnes. Two illustrative changeovers of 1.5 hours each reduce effective capacity to 141.25 tonnes, producing approximately 98.1% modeled effective utilization and only 2.75 tonnes of headroom. An eight-hour packaging breakdown reduces effective capacity to 131.25 tonnes and creates a 7.25-tonne shortfall. A modeled six-hour overtime response recovers 7.5 tonnes, restoring capacity to 138.75 tonnes and leaving a small 0.25-tonne buffer.

The report explains the planning process, master-data and configuration considerations, scheduling simulation, bottleneck logic, recovery alternatives, ERP–SAP SCM integration framework, KPI controls, twelve proposed validation tests, risks, governance, and a 33-hour work plan. Editable diagrams and supporting analytical documents are included in the repository.

All quantities, capacities, schedules, breakdown events, recovery calculations, and KPI outcomes are illustrative. The simulated PP/DS planning-board view is clearly identified as a learning representation and not a live SAP screenshot. The assessment therefore demonstrates practical planning reasoning without claiming production-system execution.
